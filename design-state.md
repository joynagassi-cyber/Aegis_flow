# Design State: Dashboard Personnelle

## Summary
- **Problem**: Lack of aesthetic cohesion and clear visual hierarchy for a highly disciplined 360-degree management system.
- **Primary Persona**: Commander VIP.
- **Success Metric**: Clear visual correlation between daily discipline (socle) and high-level KPIs (Spirituality & SaaS).

## Design Principles
- **Integrity**: Every data point serves a pillar.
- **Focus**: High-level KPIs are prominent; daily routine is accessible yet secondary.
- **Clarity**: Visual noise reduction.
